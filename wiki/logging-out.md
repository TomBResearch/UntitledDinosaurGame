# Logging out

Your character is saved as you play, so you can stop whenever you like.

## Where you come back

When you log back in, you appear where you were standing when you left, with the same health. If you left in the middle of a walk, you appear where you'd got to.

Your character is saved:
- when you log out or close the game;
- if your connection drops;
- every minute or so while you're playing;
- when the world restarts.

## Being idle

If you don't do anything for **15 minutes** (no clicks, and nothing typed in chat), you're logged out automatically, and saved where you stand.

- **One minute before**, a message in the chat box warns you: "You'll be logged out in 1 minute unless you do something."
- Click somewhere or say something to stay in.
- Moving the camera doesn't count, and nor does a long walk you started with one click.

## World restarts

Worlds sometimes restart for updates. If you're playing when that happens:
- the chat box tells you: "The world is restarting in 1 minute. Your progress will be saved.";
- you can keep playing during that minute;
- then you're saved and disconnected, and you can log back in once the world is back.

## One character at a time

You can only have one character online at a time, on any world. If you try to log in while one of your characters is still online, you'll be told it's already online.

If your game closed suddenly, your character may count as online for up to a minute. Wait a moment and try again.
