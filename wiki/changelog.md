# Changelog

What's changed in each update, newest first.

## In development

- You can create an Untitled Games account, log in and reset your password on the home page, then press Play. One account works in every Untitled game.
- You can chat with players within 40 metres of you: press Enter to type. See [Chat](chat.md).
- You can walk around a test area by clicking the ground, and see other players walking too. See [Controls](controls.md).
