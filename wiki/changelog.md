# Changelog

What's changed in each update, newest first.

## In development

- After pressing Play you see your characters. Make up to 3, each with a name and a look (body, skin, hair, hair colour and beard), starting in a pelt loincloth. You can rename and delete them too, and other players see your look. See [Characters](characters.md).
- You pick a world from a list that shows how busy each one is. World 1 is free; World 2 is for Premium members. See [Worlds and Premium](worlds-and-premium.md).
- You can create an Untitled Games account, log in and reset your password on the home page, then press Play. One account works in every Untitled game.
- You can chat with players within 40 metres of you: press Enter to type. See [Chat](chat.md).
- You can walk around a test area by clicking the ground, and see other players walking too. See [Controls](controls.md).
