# Worlds and Premium

## Worlds

When you log in and pick a [character](characters.md), you pick a **world** to play on. Every world has the same map, but different players. Pick a quiet world for peace, or a busy one to be around people.

The world list shows how busy each world is (**Low**, **Medium**, **High** or **Full**), and which worlds are for Premium members. A full or offline world can't be joined just now: pick another or try again soon.

| World | Who can play |
|---|---|
| World 1 | Everyone |
| World 2 | Premium members |

- Your character is the same on every world. Your skills, items and bank come with you.
- You can be on one world at a time.
- You can have up to **3 characters** on your account.

## Premium

Your account works across all Untitled Games. Premium is part of that account.

**Right now, Premium gives you access to premium-only worlds.** That's all. The whole starting world is free to play, and Premium gives no gameplay advantage.

Premium-only areas and content may arrive in future expansions.
