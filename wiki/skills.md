# Skills

You can train any skill you like, in any order. Every skill goes from level 1 to [level 100](levels-and-xp.md).

## Combat

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Strength** | Fight up close with clubs, spears and blades. Higher Strength means you hit more often and harder. |
| **Ranged** | Fight from a distance with slingshots and throwing weapons. |
| **Voodoo** | Cast spells with staffs and totems. |
| **Defence** | Avoid getting hit, and wear tougher armour. |
| **Hitpoints** | How much damage you can take. It goes up as you fight. |

## Gathering

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Digging** | Dig up fossils, teeth, bones and buried relics. |
| **Lumberjacking** | Chop trees for logs. |
| **Excavating** | Mine rocks for flint, stone and rarer minerals. |
| **Angling** | Catch fish from rivers, lakes and the sea. |

## Processing

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Crafting** | Make clothing, armour, jewellery and tools. |
| **Weapon-Crafting** | Make clubs, spears, slingshots, voodoo staffs and ammo. |
| **Herbology** | Gather herbs and brew potions. |
| **Cooking** | Cook food. Eating cooked food is how you heal. |

## Other

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Hunting** | Take hunting tasks from a Hunt Master, track down the creatures they name, and earn rewards. Some creatures can only be hunted at a high enough Hunting level. |
