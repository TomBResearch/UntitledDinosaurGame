# Controls

You play with the mouse.

| To… | Do this |
|---|---|
| Walk somewhere | Left-click the ground. A yellow ring marks the spot until you get there. |
| Turn the camera | Hold the middle mouse button (press the wheel) and drag, or hold the arrow keys |
| Zoom in or out | Scroll the mouse wheel |

Your character finds its own way around rocks and up slopes, and walks at the same pace for everyone, so other players see you exactly where you are.
