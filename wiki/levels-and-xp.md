# Levels and XP

Every skill starts at level 1 and goes up to **level 100**. You earn XP (experience) each time you successfully do something with a skill: chop a log, cook a fish, land a hit. When you have enough XP, you level up.

- Early levels come quickly. You'll reach level 10 in a new skill within minutes.
- Each level needs a bit more XP than the one before.
- Level 92 is roughly halfway to 100 in XP, so the last levels are a real achievement.
- XP keeps counting after level 100, up to 200 million.

Your **total level** is all your skill levels added together.
