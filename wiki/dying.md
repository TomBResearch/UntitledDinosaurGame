# Dying

Dinosaurs bite. If your health reaches zero, you're knocked out, and after a few seconds you wake up at the nearest camp fire with full health.

**You keep everything:** your items, your gear and all your XP. The only things you lose are time, the walk back, and any potion boosts you had active.
