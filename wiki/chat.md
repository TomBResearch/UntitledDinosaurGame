# Chat

Talk to the cavemen around you.

## How to chat

1. Press **Enter**. A box opens at the bottom left of the screen.
2. Type your message and press **Enter** to say it. Press **Esc** to change your mind.

While you're typing, you can still click the ground to walk.

What you say shows in the chat box at the bottom left, and above your head for a few seconds.

## Who hears you

Chat is **local**: everyone within **40 metres** of you hears it, and nobody further away does. That's about ten seconds' walk. If someone can't hear you, walk closer.

## The rules

- A message can be up to **120 characters** long.
- You can send about **one message a second**. You can send a quick burst of three, but after that, wait a moment between messages. If you send lots more, too fast, the game disconnects you.
- Rude words are hidden with stars (`****`).
- Players who break the rules can be muted. If you're muted, nobody hears you, and the chat box tells you so.
