# Characters

Your account can have up to **3 characters**. After you log in and press Play, you see them in a list: pick one to see them turn, then press Play to choose a world.

## Making a character

Pick an empty slot, give your character a name, and choose how they look:

| Part | Choices |
|---|---|
| Body | Sturdy, Lean, Broad |
| Skin | Six skin tones, light to dark |
| Hair | Shaggy, Short, Long, Topknot, Bald |
| Hair colour | Black, Dark brown, Brown, Auburn, Blond, Grey |
| Beard | None, Stubble, Short, Long |

Use the arrows to go through each one, or press **Randomise**. Every character starts in a pelt loincloth. Everyone in the world sees your character the way you made them.

## Names

- **3 to 12 characters**: the letters A to Z and the numbers 0 to 9, with single spaces between words. Accented letters and symbols aren't allowed.
- Every name is unique. Capital letters don't make a name different, so if someone is called Grug, nobody else can be grug.
- Rude names, and names that look like staff, aren't allowed.

If a name can't be used, the game tells you why, and you can try another.

## Renaming

You can rename a character **once every 30 days**. Nobody else can take the old name for 30 days after you change it, so you can change your mind and take it back.

## Deleting

To delete a character, type their name to confirm. **This can't be undone.** The name stays reserved for 7 days afterwards.

You can't rename or delete a character while they're in a world.

## Playing

Only one of your characters can be in a world at a time. Your character is the same on every world: see [Worlds and Premium](worlds-and-premium.md).
