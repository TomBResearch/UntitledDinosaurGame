# The bank

Your pockets only hold so much. Visit a **Stash Cave** at a camp to store items in your bank.

- Your bank is shared by every world, so it's always the same bank.
- Everything stacks in the bank, so 500 logs take one space.
- You can deposit or withdraw 1, 5, 10, a custom amount, or everything.
